## Full description (Play Store up to 4000 char., App Store up to 4000 char.)

No more doing the maths on your phone after dinner.

Take a photo of the receipt (or import one from your gallery, or a scanned PDF) and dividirconta uses AI to automatically recognise each item and its price — no manual typing needed.

Then it's simple:
• Create groups (a family, a couple, each person individually — whatever fits the occasion)
• Assign each item to a group with a tap, or split an item across several groups when it's shared (like a bottle of wine)
• See the exact total each group owes, with food and drinks broken down separately
• Share the final summary by message

Perfect for group dinners, shared grocery runs, trips, or any time a bill needs to be split fairly — nobody overpays, nobody underpays.

Works in English and Portuguese. Your saved groups stay ready to reuse next time.

Your data stays on your phone — no accounts, no sign-up, no ads.
